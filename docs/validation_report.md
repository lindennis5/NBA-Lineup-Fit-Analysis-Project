# Validation status

The four-season extraction and seven notebooks completed. Team-level source reconciliation passes for seconds, offensive/defensive possessions, points and opponent points. Original pilot coefficients and standard errors reproduce exactly. This is not a declaration that every source field is correct.

League conservation fails for 2024–25 (points −56; possessions −2) and 2025–26 (possessions −1,850). Those seasons and pooled results remain provisional. The 2022–23 and 2023–24 balanced-counter subset is reported separately. Official 2022–23 team games and scoring match all 30 teams; other seasons and lineup-level assignments are not externally certified.

See [season status](season_validation_status.csv), [official reconciliation](official_reconciliation.csv), [pilot reproduction](pilot_reproduction.csv), [test results](test_results.txt), and [full report](research_report.md). Native Jupyter-kernel and remote CI execution are not claimed.
